# Robot overview

Each team owns a robot, a build season, and a folder in the team's logs.

## Kunai

A compact, fast drivetrain with a low center of gravity. Kunai favors tight
turning and quick cycle times.

## Atlatl

Built around a long-range launcher. Atlatl's design notes focus on arm
geometry and consistent shot placement.

## Hunga Munga

A heavyweight platform with a modular manipulator — swappable between matches.

## Slingshot

Lightest of the four, with a tension-based intake and a very short cycle time.
